# Listening and Active Communication

## Active Listening

### Introduction

The act of fully hearing and and comphrehending the meaning of what someone is saying.

### What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

- Avoid getting distracte by your own thoughts.
- Focus on the speaker and the topic.
- Try not to interrupt the other person while they talk.
- Let the person finish talking before responding.
- Use door openers.
- Listen with body language.
- Take notes if important person.
- Paraphrase.

## Reflective Listening

### According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?
 
- Listen carefully without interrupting.
- Understand the speaker's thoughts and feelings.
- Repeat what you understood.
- Ask questions.
- Confirm that you understood correctly.

## Reflection

### What are the obstacles in your listening process?

- Distracted by my own thoughts.
- Low attention span.
- Listening to only respond.

### What can you do to improve your listening?

- Staying focused.
- Being more patient.

## Types of Communication

### When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day to day life?

- When I find it difficult to say no.
- When I hesitate to express my feelings.

### When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day to day life?

- When I get frustrated and raise my voice.
- When I react rudely out of anger.
  
### When do you switch into Passive Aggressive (sarcasm/gossiping/taunts/silent treatment and others) communication styles in your day to day life?

- When I use sarcasm or taunts instead of speaking directly.
- When I express frustration through indirect comments.

### How can you make your communication assertive?

- Express my thoughts clearly and confidently.
- Staying calm during disagreements.
- Setting boundaries and communicate them respectfully.
