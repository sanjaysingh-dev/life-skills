# Learning Process

## How to Learn Faster with the Feynman Technique

### The Feynman Technique

Explaining what you're learning in simple language like you were teachning it to someone else.

## How to Learn TED talk by Barbara Oakley

### Most interesting story or idea

The ideology that fast thinkers are like race cars, they zoom straight to the answer but they can easily miss the small details along the way whereas slow thinkers are more like hikers who take longer to get there, but because they trek through the rough spots by hand, they end up understanding the whole landscape much better.

### What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

- Focus mode - Direct and concentrated attention

- Diffused mode - A relaxed neural state that activated during breaks that allows creative breakthroughs.

## Learn Anything in 20 hours
