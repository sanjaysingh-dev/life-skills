### Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

- Unwanted physical contact.
- Making dirty jokes or comments.
- Sending inappropriate messages or pictures.
- Staring or making someone feel uncomfortable.
- Asking for sexual favours.

## What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

- Tell the person to stop directly.
- Report it to HR or the upper management.
- Support the person facing harassment.
- Keep the matter private and cooperate with the investigation.
