# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Grit

### Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.

Success is not just about being talented or smart. It’s about working hard, staying consistent, and not giving up when things get difficult.

## Introduction to Growth Mindset

### Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.

A growth mindset means believing that you can improve your skills and abilities through learning and practice. Instead of giving up when things get difficult, learn from your mistakes and keep trying.

## Understanding Internal Locus of Control

### What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

Internal Locus of Control: Believing that what happens in your life mostly depends on your own actions and decisions.

Key point: Don't blame luck or other people for your failures. Focus on what you can control, take responsibility, and work towards improving yourself.

## How to build a Growth Mindset

### What are the key points mentioned by speaker to build growth mindset (explanation not needed).

- Believe that you can learn and improve.
- Take on new challenges.
- Don't be afraid of making mistakes.
- Learn from failures.
- Accept feedback from others.
- Keep practising and putting in effort.
- Don't give up easily.
- Have a positive attitude towards learning.
